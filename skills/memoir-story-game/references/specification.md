# Memoir game specification

Build a personal introduction as a playable side-scrolling journey through the author's memories. The player controls the author and moves between scenes, approaching characters or memory markers to explore the author's experiences.

The skill has exactly two steps:

1. Chapters from memoir-composer plus events from memoir-author-timeline become a full source-linked scene pool with recommendations by life stage. Preserve scope, supported chronology, uncertainty and attribution. Pause for the owner to choose options and count, within the runtime's 128-option bound, with a clear cost notice.
2. The confirmed subset becomes a creative brief on the composer model. The host passes that brief and the exact selected scenes to the separate, independently updatable `higgsfield-websites --type game` skill, which runs on Astra. Validate its Memoir HTML/CSS export and use the host's movement/interaction engine, with distinct settings, connected travel, keyboard/touch controls, readable dialogue, reduced motion and a complete text version.

Creative presentation is welcome; personal facts remain grounded in supplied chapters and timeline events. The generated page uses generic visual representations and the author's account rather than invented testimony from represented people.

The scene choice is the required human decision before building. Publication and person-permission review are not additional phases. The host saves successful output and presents it immediately for private play. Private workspace access does not authorize public sharing or Higgsfield deployment.
