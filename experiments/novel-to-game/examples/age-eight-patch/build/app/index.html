The remembered lesson stays fixed

Dad taught me in the shed behind our rented house, with tools in a blue biscuit tin. The first patch lifted at one edge. We tried again on another Saturday; eventually a patch stayed flat. I cannot remember the month.

What I remember beyond the shed

During our two-night stay with Grandma in the heavy rain, Mum checked home and told me my drawings were dry. After the school year ended, and after both the play and that stay, I planted beans with Mum. A seedling opened by the next morning after she asked me to leave it alone. I drew it in a book that had stayed dry.

These memories are not rewards caused by a repair.
