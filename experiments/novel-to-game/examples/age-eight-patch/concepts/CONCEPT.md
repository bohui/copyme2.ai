# CONCEPT — A Patch That Stays

## 1. Locks and open dimensions
Locked: quick mode; English (Australia), retrospective first person; fictional-example labelling; general audience; one 3–5 minute offline browser prototype; keyboard, pointer and touch; untimed button controls, accessible text and reduced motion; original CSS/SVG and system fonts; deterministic local HTML/CSS/JavaScript without services or storage; complete memory view, ending and restart. Current host delivery and stage boundaries supersede older brief details.

Open: focal activity, genre and presentation. Select **system-led**, replacing the provisional hybrid profile.

## 2. Experience promise
Make a small practical task understandable through observation, help and another attempt—not by changing childhood.

Observable success: the player uses bubble evidence, diagnoses an unsuccessful patch and produces a visibly flat practice repair. Failure: progress means only advancing text, inspection is irrelevant, or practice performance rewrites remembered events.

## 3. Precedent and validation form
Borrow close-up repair-puzzle grammar: enter a workspace, inspect, manipulate, test and revisit. Reject pixel hunting, inventory bloat and timed dexterity, not the genre’s readable goals and object feedback. C5 changes the sequence through bubbles, chalk, waiting and a lifting edge. Validate with an interactive state-based whitebox; bespoke physics are unnecessary.

## 4. Meaningful candidates
### A — A Patch That Stays: observation-and-repair puzzle
**experienceProfile:** system-led. **signature_command:** N/A.

Identity: the narrator revisiting Dad’s lesson through explicitly labelled game practice. Repeated actions: select tube regions, compare observations, mark a suspected hole, apply glue, wait, press and inspect.

Game-only bubble patterns distinguish persistent leakage from incidental surface bubbles. A labelled preparation indicator advances through deliberate Wait actions, never real-time delay. Pressure comes from incomplete evidence and the temptation to press early, not a clock.

Testing reads back the actual attempt: a misplaced patch leaves bubbles; premature pressing curls its edge; correctly placed, ready patches lie flat. Observations remain available during retries. Thus failure supplies information rather than restarting the memory.

Signature image: chalk beside rising bubbles, then a flat patch beside the blue biscuit tin. Minimum slice: one complete inspect–mark–prepare–test loop. Biggest risk: rote button sequencing. Veto observation: blind repetition works as reliably as reading evidence.

### B — Keeping My Place: guided-reading practice
**experienceProfile:** system-led. **signature_command:** N/A.

Borrow line-guide interfaces; reject speed tests and literacy grades. As the narrator practising with cardboard, move the guide, follow a line and recover after a place-shift. Alignment visibly preserves continuity; misalignment offers repositioning. Pressure is maintaining place, not pronunciation.

Signature image: one clear line above cardboard. Minimum slice: one explicitly invented practice page, never attributed to the remembered boat book. Biggest risk: evaluating the player’s reading rather than supporting attention. Veto observation: success reduces to repetitive Next presses or reading assessment.

## 5. Vetoes, trade-offs and selection
Both directions can close consequential loops without unavailable assets or altered biography. Neither is ruled out structurally; each retains its stated behavioural veto.

**Quick-mode selection: A.** Its evidence, resistance, retry and visible repair are more concrete. B connects closely to remembered assistance but needs more invented prose and risks making difficulty the spectacle. A reuses one workspace with lower content cost.

## 6. Agency contract and complete slice
One guided practice tube leads to one independent layout, then a designed ending showing the repaired practice tube, attempt-specific feedback and restart. These are game exercises, not additional Saturdays. Historical first-lift/later-flat outcomes stay fixed even after immediate player success.
