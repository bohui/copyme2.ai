# ART DIRECTION — The Blue Gate

**targetFinish:** playable-prototype · **experienceProfile:** hybrid · English (Australia).

## Identity
Quick selection: original cut-paper neighbourhood over pixel tiles or watercolour wash: clearer silhouettes and cues. Warm paper, ink outlines, cobalt gate, muted sage and ochre; no distressed textures. Generic figures and Hobart scenery, without likenesses, period claims or cultural shorthand. Keep “Fictional example” and “Memory paths, not dates”. Neither palette nor backdrop assigns seasons to uncertain memories.

## Composition and world
Enlarge the desktop playfield within an approximately 1100px page, preserving logical geometry, horizontal travel and camera. Fill empty sky with restrained tree/roof/shelf shapes; repeat ground seams, fence slats and verge tufts along sparse paths. Scenery must move with the existing world, never obscure labels or introduce destinations.

Author: short, high-contrast silhouette, separated head/legs, bright jacket edge and readable stride. Adults taller; Sam is a same-scale child classmate. Identify NPC roles through objects and labels, not facial likeness. Keep encounter anchors, player foreground and cues unobstructed; side-by-side composition conveys companionship rather than confrontation.

Source-supported anchors across all encounters:
- Home: blue timber gate, window/plum tree, saucepan, floor meal.
- School: creek footbridge/boards and bag; classroom window/blackboard; cardboard reading guide/boat-book motif; low wall, apple/bottle tops; hall’s cardboard tree, painted sun and coat.
- Shed: blue biscuit tin, tube/basin, chalk and patch.
- Outings: envelope list, string bag, apples/potatoes; library card, bird book and stamp; Grandma’s towels, chair, schoolbag and blanket.
- Garden: fence, sticks, beans and drawing; bent/open seedling follows existing phase.

## Interface and feedback
Hierarchy: location, explored count, nearby action, instructions. Hide developer `#position` only; retain IDs/data hooks. Proximity uses a strong amber bracket plus Read/Travel wording. Explored markers gain a filled/check-marked token, not colour alone; changes follow existing credit rules. Destination arrows remain legible during travel; address overshoot through visibility, never snapping or slowing. Wrong turns/revisits remain neutral, not failure.

System sans-serif, 16–18px reading text, contrast target ≥4.5:1. Preserve untimed, scrollable dialogue, focus restoration and all ten unchanged full-text passages. Instant text, no typewriter; Close permits exit and full-text revisits. No invented testimony or visual claims resolving source uncertainties. Never depict coin recovery or creek flooding. No public chapter/timeline/source identities.

At 320px, reflow secondary information, keep prose unscaled and all three touch controls ≥56px. Strong dual-outline focus and pressed states; simplify decoration before sacrificing figure/cue legibility.

## Key moments
Gate departure: striding author, receding gate and ground. Approach: stable cue precedes reading; closing changes marker/progress. Finale: preserve nine-memory unlock, tenth account, return walk, labelled next morning, opened seedling/drawing and “Journey complete — The Blue Gate”. Restart restores initial framing and every visual state. No cutscene replacement or premature opening.

Reduced motion removes bob/easing/decorative animation, not world/camera travel. Silent: no voice, music or audio-dependent signals.

## Scope, budget and evidence
Essential: silhouettes, gate, scene anchors, cue/progress/ending states, readable controls. Degradable: foliage and paper layering. Original CSS on approved markup/existing inline SVG; system fonts only. No markup/JavaScript/rule changes, new scenes, mini-games or external media.

Budget: this direction call, one CSS-only production pass, fresh final QA; one necessary repair available. Usage remains receipt-measured, not a hard compute promise.

Supplied browser feedback reports desktop/phone whitebox PASS and ten-memory completion; not rerun here. Preserve the failed Enter-on-Right receipt and verified keyboard-button repair.
