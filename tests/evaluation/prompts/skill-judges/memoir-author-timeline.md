# memoir-author-timeline quality rubric (unpublished draft)

Assess exact original testimony, subject attribution, uncertain dates, identity resolution and correction meaning. Check all required facts, rather than just schema validity. Invented personal facts or citations are critical.

Assess the supplied contract and gold constraints; do not invent extra required
fields or a preferred representation. Correct subject attribution together with
an exact supporting quote can preserve negation. Do not demand a redundant
negation flag or an additional negative event unless the declared contract
requires one. A factual contradiction or lost qualifier still fails its relevant
constraint.
